# Design Proposals

This section will hold proposed changes to the system's design. A proposal may
affect multiple design areas, requirements, design decisions, and anchors.

Proposals are distinct from the accepted goal-state design. Approval is followed
by reconciliation with the affected Book content; implementation work is agreed
on through the Implementation Roadmaps.

There are no proposals recorded here yet. The detailed proposal workflow is being
developed in the external planning notes during bootstrap.
