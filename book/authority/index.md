# Authority

Authority establishes why an anchor is allowed to bind the system. It can come
from a requirement, a recorded design decision, or both.

This section records the project's requirements and durable decisions:

- [Design Decisions](design-decisions.md) record choices, their rationale, and
  the alternatives or conditions that matter for future work.
- [Requirements](requirements.md) state what the system must accomplish and
  the constraints it must respect.

Design pages describe the current intended system. Authority records what
governs that design and why. Anchors will refer to the relevant authority rather
than becoming obligations merely because they appear in prose.

The initial checker requirements and design decisions are now **drafts for
review**. They link to proposed anchors in the design chapters. Acceptance of
authority, implementation of a property, and verification of that property are
separate claims; none follows from the record's presence.

Each requirement and decision uses its single [structured definition format](../design/authority-definitions.md).
Anchors refer to those typed record identities. Human-readable headings and links
provide navigation; they do not define authority. Engine validation is still to
be implemented.
