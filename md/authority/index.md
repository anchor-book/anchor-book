# Authority

Authority establishes why an anchor is allowed to bind the system. It can come
from a requirement, a recorded design decision, or both.

This section will hold the project's requirements and durable decisions:

- [Design Decisions](design-decisions.md) record choices, their rationale, and
  the alternatives or conditions that matter for future work.
- [Requirements](requirements.md) state what the system must accomplish and
  the constraints it must respect.

Design pages describe the current intended system. Authority records what
governs that design and why. Anchors will refer to the relevant authority rather
than becoming obligations merely because they appear in prose.

The initial requirements and decision records have not yet been transferred into
this Book.
